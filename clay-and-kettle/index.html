02Our story

Our story

Clay and Kettle began as a quiet idea: make brewing at home feel like a small ceremony. We bring simple, well-made objects into domestic moments, encouraging slow attention and gentle ritual. Designed for giving or keeping, our pieces are meant to sit naturally in your daily life.

— Clay and Kettle
